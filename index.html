Solar is powerful.
Keeping it that way
takes attention.

Dust, heat and changing sunlight can all affect how a solar installation performs. Checking and maintaining panels takes time — especially when useful information is hard to see.

SolarIQ is being built around a simple idea: bring sensing and practical automation together, so panel owners can make more informed maintenance decisions.

Dust build-up

Surface dust can obstruct sunlight and call for regular cleaning.

☼

Changing sunlight

A fixed panel angle may not capture the best available sunlight throughout the day.

⌁

Limited visibility

Without regular readings, changes in temperature and electrical output can be easy to miss.

A smarter solar companion.
